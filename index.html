Why UPVC

Made to last.

Water resistant

Does not swell or rot with water. Ideal for kitchens and wet areas.

Termite free

No wood inside, so termites and borers are not a problem.

Built-in lighting

Profile lights, spot lights and cove LEDs planned with every design.

Many finishes

High gloss, matte, marble and wood-grain looks in many colours.
